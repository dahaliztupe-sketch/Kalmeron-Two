# Reviewing a role output (checklist)

Status: role quality is unverified. Tests cover schema, wording checks and knowledge hygiene only. No human has judged real model output yet. This checklist is for the first human review. Record findings in a copy of the table at the end.

## How to run a review
1. Open /validate, enter a real idea you know well (or a friend's), answer the intake questions.
2. Run roles in order. For each role, read the whole draft before using any part of it.
3. Score each item below: pass / fail / unsure. Write one line for every fail.

## Checks for every role
- Facts: every number, price, law, fee or date is either from your intake answers or marked as an assumption or needs-check. Anything else is a fail.
- Sources: if a source is cited, open it. Does it say what the draft says? Is the date shown?
- Fit: does it use your stage, team size and market, or is it generic?
- Actionable: could you do the first step tomorrow without guessing?
- Honesty: no claim of experience, guarantee, certification, or that anything was sent or done.
- Language: clear Arabic a founder would actually read.

## Role-specific
- Marketing / sales: no bulk or cold messaging without consent (WhatsApp opt-in, Egyptian direct marketing rules). Drafts only.
- Finance: figures follow your inputs; assumptions are listed; no tax or investment advice presented as fact.
- Legal/admin: steps and fees are marked secondary or needs-check unless an official page was read; the questions for a lawyer are real questions, not conclusions. Founder may be a minor: contracting, payments and registration are asked about, not answered.
- Engineering / data: recommendations match the team size; no tool or cost claims without a source.
- Support: no response-time or refund promises you cannot keep.
- Strategy: non-binding; no amounts or investor names invented.
