# First founder pilot (draft plan, owner decides)

Who: a founder with a new idea (default segment). Small business owner is the second segment, later.
Format: hand-held free pilot, one to three founders, run by the owner. Not a public launch. No payment in the pilot (the owner has no payment rail and the age/legal side is open).

## Path
1. Recruit: people the owner already knows. No cold or bulk messages (consent rules, see marketing/legal knowledge).
2. Explain plainly: AI-drafted plans, not advice, may be wrong. Ask permission to read their outputs for feedback.
3. Founder runs /validate on their idea, then the roles. Owner sits with them or calls after.
4. Ask the past-behaviour questions: what did they do with the plan this week? what did they ignore and why?
5. Record: time to first useful output, which roles were used, what was wrong (use the rubric in REAL_OUTPUT_REVIEW.md).
6. Data: tell them how to delete a saved plan; honour any deletion request the same day.
7. Decide after 3 founders: keep, change, or stop. Price discussion only after real use.

## Open decisions for the owner
- Which three people to ask first.
- Whether he reads their outputs (needs their consent).
- Price, payment rail and what he can legally accept at his age (not yet decided).
- Whether roles may ever act externally (currently drafts only).
